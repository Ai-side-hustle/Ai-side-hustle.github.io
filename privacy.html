Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing from viewers. The tool is used only by the channel owner. When the owner connects a TikTok or YouTube account, the platform returns an access token and the account's basic public information (for TikTok: open_id, display name and avatar under user.info.basic), used only to confirm the right account is connected.

How it is used

Tokens are used only to upload the channel's own videos to the owner's own account, with their caption and the AI-generated label.

Where it is kept

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable by that user alone. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold or shared. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads; it loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Deletion

The owner can disconnect at any time in the platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) and by deleting the token file.
